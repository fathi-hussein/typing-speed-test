And regarding semicolons: JavaScript has a feature called Automatic Semicolon Insertion (ASI) where the engine tries to guess where semicolons go. Sometimes it works, but other times it can lead to silent, frustrating bugs (especially when lines start with parentheses, square brackets, or template literals). Getting into the habit of adding them—or adopting a formatter like Prettier—will save you hours of debugging later down the line.
